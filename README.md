# Evolutionary Reorganization of Transcriptomic Architecture Across a UVB Tolerance Gradient in Fish

This repository contains the computational scripts associated with the study:

**Valdivieso, C. & Allende, M. L. (2026). Evolutionary Reorganization of Transcriptomic Architecture Across a UVB Tolerance Gradient in Fish. _Ecology and Evolution_, 16(9), e74298.**

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.74298

## Overview

The study investigates how transcriptomic responses to ultraviolet-B (UVB) exposure differ across fish models representing distinct levels of physiological protection and evolutionary adaptation to UVB stress.

The analysis compares:

- **Wild-type zebrafish (*Danio rerio*)**
- **nacre zebrafish**, a melanin-deficient mutant
- ***Orestias ascotanensis***, a high-altitude Andean killifish naturally exposed to high levels of UVB radiation

RNA-seq data were used to characterize transcriptional responses, which were subsequently analyzed in the context of protein–protein interaction networks, orthology, and positive selection.

The study reports differences in both the magnitude of transcriptional responses and the organization of transcriptomic networks across the UVB tolerance gradient.
